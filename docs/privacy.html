Privacy Policy for Pennora

Last Updated & Effective: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pennora ("we", "us", or "our"), an offline-first personal finance tracking application developed by Abhishek (Application ID: com.abhishekkx.pennora), handles information when you use our mobile application.

🔒 Offline-First & Privacy-By-Default Pennora is designed with a strict offline-first architecture. Your financial records, expense logs, budget caps, and personal notes remain exclusively on your physical device. We do not operate external database servers or track user accounts.

1. Information Collection and Storage

1.1 Local Data Storage (On-Device)

All transaction histories, custom category preferences, budget limits, and settings entered into Pennora are stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on your device using the Drift database framework.

  • No External Database Servers: We do not own, operate, or transmit your financial logs to any external cloud databases or centralized servers.
  • No Accounts Required: Pennora does not require you to create an account, log in with an email address, or provide personal identifiable information (PII).

1.2 Google Gemini AI Feature ("Talk to Pocket")

Pennora includes an optional AI financial assistant feature ("Talk to Pocket"). When you explicitly ask questions or interact with the AI assistant:

  • Data Transmitted: The App packages your local financial database context (e.g., spending categories, total expenditure, income figures) along with your query and securely transmits it via encrypted HTTPS directly to the Google Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com).
  • Purpose: This data is sent solely to generate instant, personalized financial advice and analysis requested by you.
  • Third-Party Processing: Processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google AI Terms of Service. We do not store your prompt history or financial queries on any developer servers.

2. Device Permissions Used

  1. android.permission.INTERNET: Required to communicate with the Google Gemini API when you explicitly use the AI assistant feature and to load web typography fonts. Never used for background tracking or advertising.
  2. android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to post local budget pacing notifications and daily reminders generated locally by your device.

3. Data Safety & Analytics

  • Zero Ads & Zero Trackers: Pennora contains no advertising SDKs, marketing trackers, or third-party profiling scripts.
  • No Financial Credentials: Pennora NEVER requests bank account numbers, credit card details, PINs, passwords, or online banking log-ins.

4. User Data Control & Deletion Instructions

Because all data is stored locally on your physical device, you have 100% control over your data at all times:

How to Delete 100% of Your Data:
  1. In-App Reset: Open Settings inside Pennora and tap "Reset All Data". This permanently deletes all database records instantly.
  2. Device Settings Clear: Open your Android device Settings -> Apps -> Pennora -> Storage & Cache -> Clear Storage.
  3. Uninstall Application: Uninstalling Pennora permanently removes the local SQLite database and all associated files.

5. CSV Data Export

Pennora allows you to export your transaction logs to standard CSV files. Generated CSV files are saved locally on your device and shared only through native Android system share sheets when explicitly initiated by you.

6. Children's Privacy

Pennora is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
