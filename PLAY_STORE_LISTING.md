# Google Play Store Listing Copy & Graphics Guide

This document contains production-ready copy, metadata, and graphic asset specifications for publishing **Pennora** on the Google Play Store.

---

## 1. App Store Metadata

* **App Name:** Pennora - AI Expense Tracker
* **Package Name:** `com.abhishekkx.pennora`
* **Default Language:** English (United States) - `en-US`
* **Category:** Finance
* **Tags:** Personal Finance, Budget Tracker, Expense Manager, AI Financial Advisor, Money Tracker, Offline Finance

## 3. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

```text
Pennora is a modern, ultra-sleek, privacy-focused personal finance manager designed to give you complete control over your money. Inspired by world-class fintech interfaces, Pennora combines offline-first security with intelligent budget pacing, goal tracking, and an on-device AI financial coach powered by Google Gemini.

Key Features:

🔒 100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE-FIRST
Your financial records stay on your phone. Pennora uses a local SQLite database to store all transactions, budgets, and settings locally. No accounts, no sign-ins, and zero cloud tracking.

🤖 TALK TO POCKET — YOUR AI FINANCIAL COACH
Have questions about your spending habits? Ask "Pocket," your personal AI financial advisor. Pocket analyzes your local spending patterns in real-time using Google Gemini to provide instant, tailored actionable advice—without storing your data on external servers.

📊 INTUITIVE ANALYTICS & HEALTH SCORES
Visualise your spending with fluid Bezier trend charts. Instantly track your financial health score, essential needs ratio, and savings targets with zero clutter.

⚡ DUAL SLIDER & DIRECT VALUE INPUT
Effortlessly log income and expenses. Use dynamic gradient sliders or precise numerical fields with real-time synchronized input controls.

🎯 BUDGET PACING & MILESTONE TRACKING
Set monthly expenditure caps, track daily pacing limits, and stay on top of your financial goals before overspending happens.

📁 ONE-TAP CSV EXPORT
Export your transaction logs anytime to standard CSV files (Date, Time, Category, Amount, Essential Need, Note) for backup or tax filing using native device sharing.

🌙 SLEEK DARK MODE & FINTECH UI
Designed with high-end glassmorphism, responsive micro-animations, dynamic font scaling, and fluid navigation for a delightful daily experience.

Why Choose Pennora?
• Zero Ads & Zero Subscriptions
• No Bank Credentials Required
• Complete Data Ownership
• Instant Local Notifications & Budget Alerts

Take control of your financial future today with Pennora!
```
